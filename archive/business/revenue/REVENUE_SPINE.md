# SoftFocus — Revenue Spine (v1.x)

## Purpose

This document defines how SoftFocus generates revenue
without modifying product meaning, logic, or governance.

Revenue is treated as an **operational overlay**,
not as a product feature.

---

## Core Principle

Money is exchanged for:
- analytical depth
- interpretation clarity
- reduced ambiguity

Money is NOT exchanged for:
- advice
- recommendations
- outcomes
- improvement promises

---

## Revenue Sources (v1.x)

### 1. Primary — Analytical Entitlements

Product:
- Recovery Calculator v1 — Extended Interpretation

Pricing:
- $19 (standard)
- $29 (full breakdown)

Delivery:
- entitlement-based
- audit-logged
- deterministic

---

### 2. Secondary — Interpretation Materials

Product:
- Reading Your Recovery Signal (PDF)

Purpose:
- explain how to interpret results
- reduce misreading
- avoid support load

---

## What Is Explicitly Forbidden

- subscriptions
- upsell funnels
- behavioral nudges
- urgency framing
- “next step” coercion

---

## Success Metric

Revenue success is defined as:

- stable monthly sales
- low refund rate
- zero governance violations
- no increase in support burden
