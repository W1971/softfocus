# SoftFocus — Phase A Declaration (v2.x)

## Declaration

SoftFocus formally enters **Phase A — Analytical Exploration**
for **version line v2.x**.

This phase exists **in parallel** with the closed and operational
v1.x line and does not modify it.

---

## Scope of Phase A

Phase A v2 is limited to:

- analytical axis design
- terminology definition
- pattern identification
- non-executable documentation
- measurement hypothesis formulation

No product surfaces, code, UX, or scoring
may be introduced in this phase.

---

## Separation from v1.x

v1.x remains:

- operational
- governed
- immutable
- closed for semantic changes

No logic, models, or artifacts from v1.x
may be modified or reused implicitly in v2.x.

---

## Exit Criteria from Phase A

Phase A may conclude only when:

1. A stable analytical axis is defined
2. Measurable patterns are specified
3. A new bundle contract is proposed
4. A Phase B declaration is issued

Until then, v2.x remains **non-executable**.

---

**Phase A v2 is now ACTIVE.**
