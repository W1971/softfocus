# Non-Clinical Disclaimer — SoftFocus

SoftFocus is a non-clinical, informational application.

It does not provide:
- medical advice
- psychological diagnosis
- therapy or treatment
- behavioral recommendations

SoftFocus does not replace
professional medical or psychological care.

All information presented by the app
is analytical and descriptive only.
