# App Store Review — Standard Answers
## SoftFocus Mobile

These answers are intended for the
"App Review Information" section in App Store Connect.

---

## Q: What does this app do?

SoftFocus is a non-clinical analytical application.
It presents a clear analytical statement about whether
recovery closes under sustained cognitive load.

The app does not provide advice, guidance, or recommendations.

---

## Q: Who is the target audience?

Adults experiencing prolonged cognitive load
who want analytical clarity about recovery capacity.

The app is not intended for children.

---

## Q: Does the app provide medical or psychological advice?

No.

SoftFocus does not provide medical advice,
psychological diagnosis, therapy, or treatment.
It is informational and analytical only.

---

## Q: Does the app collect or store user data?

No.

The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.
No accounts, tracking, or analytics are used.

---

## Q: Does the app require login or payment?

No.

The app works without login, accounts, or in-app purchases.

---

## Q: What happens after the user receives the result?

The app ends interaction.
There are no next steps, recommendations, or prompts.

---

## Q: How does the app comply with App Store guidelines?

- No medical claims
- No behavioral guidance
- No manipulation or retention mechanics
- Transparent non-clinical positioning
