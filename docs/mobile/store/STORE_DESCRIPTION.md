## Subtitle / Short Description

---

## Full Description

SoftFocus is an analytical mobile application designed to provide clarity
about recovery capacity under sustained cognitive load.

The app does not guide, coach, or recommend actions.
It presents a clear analytical statement about whether recovery closes
under current conditions.

SoftFocus is intended for people experiencing prolonged mental load
who want to stop guessing and understand the operating state
of their system.

---

## What the App Does

- Presents a clear analytical result
- Reduces interpretive noise
- Helps users understand whether recovery closes or not
- Ends interaction without retention mechanics

---

## What the App Does NOT Do

SoftFocus does not:
- provide medical or psychological diagnosis
- offer therapy, treatment, or coaching
- give recommendations or action plans
- track habits or behavior
- influence user decisions

The app is non-clinical and informational only.
