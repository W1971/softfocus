# SoftFocus v2 — Question Dimensions

This document translates the v2 analytical axis
into **measurable question dimensions**.

No executable question sets are defined here.

---

## Axis Reference

**load predictability → control illusion → cognitive fragmentation**

---

## Dimension A — Load Predictability

### Focus
Temporal and structural predictability of cognitive load.

### Question Intent
Detect volatility, interruption, and instability of work rhythm.

### Signals
- frequency of unexpected priority shifts
- invalidation of plans
- unclear task completion boundaries

---

## Dimension B — Control Illusion

### Focus
Responsibility without proportional influence.

### Question Intent
Detect gaps between accountability and control.

### Signals
- decisions without closure
- outcomes detached from effort
- persistent justification and alignment labor

---

## Dimension C — Cognitive Fragmentation

### Focus
Integrity of sustained thinking structures.

### Question Intent
Detect loss of continuity and synthesis.

### Signals
- frequent context switching
- reduced deep-focus tolerance
- reliance on external scaffolding

---

## Boundary

These dimensions:
- are non-clinical
- do not define scoring
- do not define UI
- do not imply intervention

They exist solely to support
future assessment design in v2.
