# SoftFocus v2 — Analytical Axis Concept

## Purpose

This document proposes a new analytical axis
for SoftFocus v2.x.

It does not define product behavior
and does not modify v1.x.

---

## Proposed Axis

**load predictability → control illusion → cognitive fragmentation**

This axis analyzes how sustained cognitive load
reshapes thinking structure rather than energy levels.

---

## Key Distinctions from v1

- No recovery scoring
- No burnout stages
- No compensation framing

v2 focuses on **structural cognitive distortion**
under prolonged uncertainty and responsibility.

---

## Intended Use (Future)

- Separate assessment line
- Independent result contracts
- No shared scoring with v1
- Optional coexistence in product

---

## Status

Concept only.
No implementation permitted until Phase A v2 is declared.
