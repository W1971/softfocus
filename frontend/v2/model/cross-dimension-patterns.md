# SoftFocus v2 — Cross-Dimension Patterns

**Status:** DESIGN-ONLY  
**Phase:** Phase A — Analytical Exploration  
**Scope:** Interpretive patterns (non-scored)  
**Last updated:** 2026-02-11

---

## Purpose

This document defines **stable cross-dimension patterns**
emerging from the interaction of:

- Dimension A — Load Predictability
- Dimension B — Control Illusion
- Dimension C — Cognitive Fragmentation

These patterns are **analytical lenses**, not diagnoses,
scores, or user-facing interpretations.

They exist to:
- guide future scoring design
- prevent naive single-dimension reading
- stabilize meaning across roles

---

## Pattern Matrix (High-Level)

| Pattern Name | Dimension A | Dimension B | Dimension C | Core Dynamic |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| Reactive Drift | High | Low–Medium | Medium | Environment unstable, control not yet overcompensated |
| Compensated Instability | High | High | Medium | Instability masked by vigilance and over-control |
| Silent Fragmentation | Low | Medium | High | Fragmentation without visible instability |
| Vigilance Trap | Medium | High | High | Control illusion sustaining cognitive fragmentation |
| Structural Drift | High | High | High | Full-axis degradation |
| Local Overload | Low | Low | Medium | Acute fragmentation without systemic drivers |

---

## Pattern Descriptions

### 1. Reactive Drift  
**A↑ / B↓ / C→**

- Unstable inputs
- Reactive execution
- Control not yet internalized

Often appears early in role transitions or crisis periods.

---

### 2. Compensated Instability  
**A↑ / B↑ / C→**

- System instability persists
- Individual compensates via vigilance
- Fragmentation delayed but accumulating

High risk of sudden C escalation.

---

### 3. Silent Fragmentation  
**A↓ / B→ / C↑**

- Environment appears stable
- Control feels acceptable
- Cognitive coherence erodes quietly

Often missed by recovery-oriented models.

---

### 4. Vigilance Trap  
**A→ / B↑ / C↑**

- Control illusion becomes primary load
- Attention locked in preventive mode
- Fragmentation sustained by over-control

Stopping feels dangerous.

---

### 5. Structural Drift  
**A↑ / B↑ / C↑**

- Instability
- Compensatory control
- Cognitive disintegration

Represents full v2-axis saturation.

---

### 6. Local Overload  
**A↓ / B↓ / C→**

- No systemic instability
- Temporary fragmentation
- Often situational or short-term

Should not be over-interpreted.

---

## Notes

- Patterns are **not exclusive**; mixed states may occur.
- No pattern implies severity, diagnosis, or action.
- Patterns are **contextual**, not prescriptive.
- Final interpretation always requires multi-dimensional reading.
