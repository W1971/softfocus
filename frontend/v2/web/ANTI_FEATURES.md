# SoftFocus v2 — Anti-Features (Never)

**Status:** DESIGN-ONLY  
**Phase:** Phase C — Analytical Consolidation  
**Surface:** Web  
**Last updated:** 2026-02-11

---

## Purpose

Define what SoftFocus v2 will **never** include.
These exclusions are part of the product.

---

## Never (Hard Prohibitions)

- Advice, recommendations, “what to do”
- Protocols, intervention frameworks, action plans
- Dashboards, analytics pages, “insights” feeds
- Habit loops, streaks, gamification, reminders
- Progress tracking or improvement metrics
- Social sharing, community, comments
- Personalization by inference or adaptation
- Funnels, retention mechanics, multiple CTAs
- SEO expansion as a product driver
- Monetization UI, upsells, locked content language

---

## Never (Analytical Prohibitions)

- A single overall score that collapses meaning
- Severity labels implying diagnosis
- Clinical language or symptom framing
- “Recovery” claims or energy prescriptions
- Promises of outcomes

---

## Never (Interaction Prohibitions)

- Popups
- Nudges
- “You’re almost there”
- “Recommended next”
- “Book a call”
- “Start a plan”

---

## Whole-Widget Integrity

If a feature creates:
- curiosity loops
- dependence
- continuation pressure

it violates the whole-widget design.
