# SoftFocus v2 — Narrative Language Canon

**Status:** DESIGN-ONLY  
**Phase:** Phase C — Analytical Consolidation  
**Surface:** Web language rules  
**Last updated:** 2026-02-11

---

## Purpose

Define a stable, minimal, human language layer that:
- communicates benefits over specs
- avoids clinical drift
- prevents “advice mode”
- keeps the product insanely simple

---

## The Voice

- calm
- precise
- non-performative
- low-entropy
- respectful

No hype. No urgency. No motivation.

---

## Core Language Moves (Allowed)

### Benefits over mechanics
Say:
- “You can see what is destabilizing your focus.”
- “You stop guessing where the pressure comes from.”
- “This clarifies whether it’s structural or situational.”

Avoid:
- “Dimension A/B/C”
- “Signal density”
- “Cross-dimension imbalance”

### Structural, not personal
Say:
- “This often emerges under sustained responsibility.”
- “This pattern tends to form when volatility is compensated by vigilance.”

Avoid:
- “You are weak / anxious / broken”
- “You need to…”

### Non-clinical boundary (short)
Use minimal statements:
- “Non-clinical.”
- “Not a diagnosis.”

No expansions. No medical comparisons.

---

## Forbidden Language

- “Next steps”
- “Recovery protocol”
- “Treatment”
- “Intervention”
- “Fix / cure”
- “Improve performance”
- “Optimize productivity”
- “Do this now”

---

## “Packages of Emphasis” (What we choose not to say)

We do not:
- teach
- coach
- prescribe
- reassure with certainty
- label with severity

We only:
- name patterns
- describe structure
- stabilize meaning

---

## Microcopy Rules

- Sentences under ~12 words when possible
- One claim per sentence
- Prefer nouns and verbs; avoid adjectives
- No exclamation marks
- No “you should”

---

## Result Language Template (Canonical)

A result should read like:

1) “What is happening” (one sentence)  
2) “What sustains it” (one sentence)  
3) “What it tends to cause” (one sentence)  
4) “Non-clinical boundary” (one short line)

No more.
